## [Unreleased]

### Added

- MVP: the `moderate_room` mesh capability and the `guide_room_lifecycle`
  domain (`room_aggregate` + four command slices: `moderate_room`,
  `note_participant_joined`, `note_participant_left`, `end_room_moderation`).
  Records lifecycle facts only (`room_formed_v1` / `participant_joined_v1` /
  `participant_left_v1` / `room_moderation_ended_v1`) -- never conversation
  content. A configurable idle timeout (default 96h) ends moderation when no
  participant has joined in the window; resets on a join, never on a leave.
  Reckon-db event store + a thin barrel_docdb read model (`active_rooms_store`)
  tracking just enough (`room_topic` + `last_joined_at`) for
  `active_rooms_reaper` to find idle rooms and re-subscribe to active ones
  after a restart, without enumerating the whole event store. `room_envelope`
  decodes macula-mcp's own room wire contract and requires a cryptographically
  verified publisher, not just a self-claimed `from`, before treating a fact
  as a real join or leave. See macula-io/hecate-mods#1.
- The scaffold, generated by `rebar3 new hecate_service`: an OTP release that
  boots on `hecate_om`, joins the mesh, and answers `/health` on 8462.
- `hecate_mods_service`, the six-callback `hecate_om_service` contract, announcing
  no capability and requesting no authority because it can do nothing yet.
- Unit tests asserting the contract's shape, including that the mesh-facing name
  and the reported version match the application's own.
- `Containerfile` building an alpine image, with macula's QUIC NIF compiled from
  source rather than fetched against a foreign libc.
- `deploy/docker-compose.yml`, the service's own run contract.
- CI: `lint-and-test` on every push and pull request, `build-and-push` to
  the registry on `main` and on `v*` tags, publishing both `:latest` and the semver
  tag.
